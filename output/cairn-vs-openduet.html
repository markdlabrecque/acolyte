Research comparison · 29 October 2026

OpenDuet is much further built.
Cairn's guarantees are a different contract.

OpenDuet already has a proactive assistant product, a web UI, connected apps and agent computers. It is not a demonstrated drop-in replacement for Cairn's narrower safety, spending and Linux operating requirements.

Bottom line. Reusing OpenDuet would buy substantial product implementation. It would still require policy changes, integration work and verified recovery behavior. MIT application code does not mean unlimited free self-hosting: the supported setup requires Intelligence and model access, with separate licensing and inference costs. [1] [12]

Evidence is static source inspection summarized in the supplied research, pinned to OpenDuet 677e0fc. No app startup, fault injection, connector authorization or security audit. Cairn is mostly specification, not a proven competing implementation. Its separate todo slice has local green checks but a pending live smoke and current review; M0–M4 acceptance remains incomplete. What OpenDuet adds today

A usable assistant product

Web chat, Tasks and Make, onboarding, triage, briefs, browser screen/takeover and connected-app OAuth. Headless Tasks, event triggers and self-scheduled follow-ups are implemented, not merely roadmap items. [1] [5]

Capabilities Cairn intentionally defers

Durable Bot profiles, group conversations and typed agent handoffs; Docker computers with browser, files and shell; personal memory and reviewed workflow learning. More capability also means more policy, data-location and operating decisions. [7] [10]

Self-hostable is not unlimited or automatically free

Application and computers

OpenDuet source is MIT. The documented quick start uses local Docker computers, with no mandatory commercial sandbox rental. Host hardware, storage and operation still cost money. Model inference remains separately supplied and billed, or locally operated. No verified fixed monthly total is available. [1] [13]

Required Intelligence

Managed free tier has 1,000 successful interactions per organization/month. PAYG is US$49/month including 10,000, then graduated usage. These are Intelligence interactions, not prepaid model tokens. A successful scheduled job can count too. One 15-minute routine is about 2,880 requests in 30 days, before chat. That is arithmetic, not observed billing. [12]

Fully local Intelligence needs a license. Developer Edition is non-production evaluation with a renewable 30-day license; production self-hosting has separate licensed terms. Local evaluation requires at least 4 CPUs, 12 GiB RAM and 40 GiB storage in addition to OpenDuet. Its supported preview is macOS Docker Desktop; Linux rootful Docker evaluation is explicitly unverified/outside that preview. Optional OpenTag, Composio, research, email ingress and voice services have their own requirements and possible costs. Live vendor terms may change. [9] [12] [14]
